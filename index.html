Player support

Need help aboard the expedition? These answers cover the notebook, your saved campaign and the controls.

How do I save and continue?

The game saves each confirmed action on your device. Choose Continue on the title screen to reopen your expedition. In the notebook, open Index → Restart points to return to the last departure or to the point before the final command, if recorded. Restoring a point discards progress made after it.

What makes time pass?

You can read the journal, open the map and review a journey without spending an hour. Check the time, food and crew requirements shown before confirming an action.

Where can I change the language or text size?

Open Settings from the title screen or the notebook index. Choose English, Brazilian Portuguese, Italian or Spanish. You can also enlarge the text, reduce motion and adjust the sound.

How do I report a bug?

Tell us which device and iOS, iPadOS or macOS version you use, which notebook page was open and what happened after your last action. Reports are public. Keep personal details out of your report.
