Privacy policy

L’isola misteriosa is an offline game from Cider Studios. We collect no personal data through the game.

Updated 24 November 2026

What the game stores

The game writes your expedition, restart points and preferences to its local app storage. Preferences include language, text size, reduced motion and sound levels. These files let you resume play. The game does not send them to Cider Studios or other companies.

Accounts, advertising and analytics

The game has no player accounts, advertising, analytics or tracking SDKs. Cider Studios does not receive identifiers, location, contacts or gameplay activity through the game.

How long records stay on your device

A new campaign replaces your current expedition and restart points. To remove all local game data, including preferences, use Delete App on your iPhone or iPad. Device backups are managed separately through your device and backup provider.

On a Mac, quit the game. In Finder, use Go to Folder to open ~/Library/Containers/ and move only the game’s container, com.ciderstudios.lisolamisteriosa, to the Trash to remove its local saves and preferences.

This website and public support

GitHub hosts these pages. Opening this site in a browser or reporting a problem on GitHub is separate from playing offline. A support issue and any information you choose to post there are public. This site uses local fonts and sets no cookies of its own.
